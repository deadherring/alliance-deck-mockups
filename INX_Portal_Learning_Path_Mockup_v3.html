Moving into wall coverings · Lesson 3 of 5

Choosing the right ink for wall coverings

Article 6-minute read · includes a 2-minute video
2 of 5 lessons complete
Wall coverings are seen up close and stay on the wall for years. That changes what matters when you choose an ink.

Start with the material

Paper, non-woven and vinyl wall coverings hold ink differently. Confirm the exact material your customer wants before you choose an ink.

Think about the room

Offices, schools and healthcare sites may have rules on odor and emissions. Ask where the wall covering will hang.

Plan for wear

Hallways and public spaces get scuffed and cleaned. Ask how the wall will be cleaned, and test your print against it.
