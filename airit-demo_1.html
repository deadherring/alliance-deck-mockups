How has AI adoption changed among commercial printers?
AIRIT
▸ Covers: commercial printers · 2023–2025 · United StatesChange in filters
Statistics
Use of generative AI among commercial printers has climbed from 23% (2023) to 62% (2025) — more than double in two years.
How this answer was built
Pooled from 3 commercial-printer studies, 2023–2025 — AI Adoption (2025, n=204) · DPS Provider (2024, n=190) · DPS Provider (2023, n=176).
Answer choices differed across these studies and were lined up to compare — see how ▸
The 2023 study asked two separate options — “Use regularly” and “Use occasionally.” These were combined to match the single “Currently use” option in the 2024 and 2025 studies.
“For which of the following do you currently use generative AI tools?”  (2025 wording; earlier studies asked a comparable question)
